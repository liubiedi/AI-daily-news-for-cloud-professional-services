# Enterprise AI Strategic Daily Brief — 26 November 2026

Singapore | Coverage: 24–26 November 2026

## Executive signal
Agent integration is becoming a governed, metered service, while sovereign AI and specialist implementation partners compete for production workloads. Regional infrastructure constraints reinforce the need for deployment flexibility.

## 1. Singapore explores offshore data-centre ecosystem
**Facts:** On 25 November IQPC Asia announced a floating data-centre event for September 2027 in Singapore. Its announcement cites Keppel's planned 25 MW floating facility, due in 2028; the event itself is not a new capacity commitment.
**Strategic significance:** Land and cooling constraints could reshape regional capacity planning.
**Opportunity:** Infrastructure commissioning and resilience assurance.
**Action:** Add offshore power, cooling and connectivity dependencies to capacity scenario planning.
**Source:** [Announcement](https://www.thailand-business-news.com/pr-news/asias-ai-race-heads-offshore-with-launch-of-floating-data-centres-in-singapore)

## 2. Reuters uncovers Chinese AI server financing records
**Facts:** On 25 November Reuters reported financing records listing up to 764 servers and modules valued at US$270 million linked to associates and family of Alice Huang. The records do not establish that the equipment contained prohibited chips or how it entered China.
**Strategic significance:** Financing records add another dimension to infrastructure provenance.
**Opportunity:** Supplier and asset assurance.
**Action:** Require documented asset provenance and legal review before committing AI infrastructure.
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/alice-huang-linked-firms-listed-270-million-ai-servers-china-records-show-2026-11-25/)

## 3. Endava becomes Anthropic Select Partner
**Facts:** Endava announced the designation on 25 November, citing certified expertise and production deployments. Its offering integrates Claude with existing enterprise applications, data and business controls; outcomes are company-reported.
**Strategic significance:** Specialist implementation partners are becoming a competitive distribution channel.
**Opportunity:** Industry-focused FDE and partner enablement.
**Action:** Define a certification and production-reference requirement for AI delivery partners.
**Source:** [Endava](https://investors.endava.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/125/endava-becomes-anthropic-select-partner-to-accelerate-ai-native-engineering-and-business-ai-value)

## 4. Google introduces governed Workspace agent access tiers
**Facts:** On 24 November Google launched standard and scaled tiers and made Workspace MCP servers generally available. Standard MCP quotas are 5,000 requests per day per project per product; agent MCP requirements take effect on 1 February 2027, with scaled billing starting 1 April.
**Why included:** Slightly older but directly affects production integration design and costs.
**Strategic significance:** Agent connectors are becoming metered and policy-governed dependencies.
**Opportunity:** Connector engineering and AgentOps FinOps.
**Action:** Inventory Workspace integrations and forecast request volume and migration effort before February.
**Source:** [Google developer documentation](https://developers.google.com/workspace/guides/quota-usage-tiers)

## 5. Cloudera introduces sovereign enterprise AI offering
**Facts:** Reporting on 26 November describes Cloudera's solution combining its data platform with Mistral open-weight models and Forge. It targets private, sovereign and air-gapped deployments with governance and data lineage; independent performance results were not disclosed.
**Strategic significance:** Sovereign AI competition extends into data and model lifecycle control.
**Opportunity:** HCS private AI integration and assurance.
**Action:** Benchmark HCS against an air-gapped data-to-model deployment acceptance checklist.
**Source:** [Express Computer](https://www.expresscomputer.in/news/cloudera-introduces-solution-for-sovereign-enterprise-data-and-ai/141717/)

## Strategic synthesis
Prioritize governed connectors, specialist delivery partners and independently testable sovereign deployment controls.
