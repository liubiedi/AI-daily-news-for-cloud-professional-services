# Enterprise AI Strategic Daily Brief — 2026-11-28

Singapore | Weekend edition | Coverage: 26–28 November 2026. Older announcements are retained only for material strategic relevance.

## Executive signal
AI investment is becoming more selective, while enterprise AI restructuring and shared-inference security expose the need for measurable business value and independently tested production controls.

## 1. Keppel secures full tenancy for 60 MW Korean AI data centre
**Facts:** Keppel announced on 26 November that kt cloud committed to the entire capacity of its planned Ansan facility. Power and construction approvals are secured, with service targeted for 2030; this is contracted future capacity, not live compute.
**Why now:** 27 November coverage highlights a significant Singapore-led regional infrastructure commitment.
**Strategic significance:** Anchor demand and secured power can reduce development uncertainty.
**Opportunity:** AI infrastructure commissioning and lifecycle assurance.
**Action:** Require separate power, equipment, tenant-demand and commissioning milestones in regional AI proposals.
[Keppel](https://www.keppel.com/media/keppel-data-centre-fund-iii-secures-kt-cloud-as-master-tenant-for-60-mw-south-korea-data-centre-/)

## 2. Fitch expects more selective Asian data-centre investment
**Facts:** Reuters reported on 26 November that Fitch expects tighter US chip controls to slow regional investment. Fitch identifies leveraged GPU-cloud operators and projects with uncertain customers or chip access as particularly exposed.
**Why now:** The credit-risk assessment adds a financing perspective to yesterday's shipment story.
**Strategic significance:** Customer concentration and supply certainty affect bankability.
**Opportunity:** Compliance-ready infrastructure and capacity assurance.
**Action:** Add financing, customer eligibility and confirmed accelerator supply to bid reviews.
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/us-chip-curbs-will-slow-asia-data-center-investment-fitch-says-2026-11-26/)

## 3. Beijing market support triggers Chinese semiconductor rally
**Facts:** Reuters reported on 27 November that the Shanghai Composite rose about 3.7% and the STAR semiconductor index 19.5% after Beijing pledged stronger state-backed market support. Analysts cautioned that lasting recovery depends on economic fundamentals.
**Strategic significance:** Improved liquidity could support domestic technology investment, but equity gains do not establish operational competitiveness.
**Opportunity:** Evaluate domestic accelerator and software ecosystem maturity.
**Action:** Track actual delivery capacity, software compatibility and customer adoption rather than equity prices.
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/markets/europe/view-china-markets-surge-beijing-unleashes-national-team-2026-11-27/)

## 4. SAP weighs major workforce reductions amid AI restructuring
**Facts:** Reuters reported on 27 November that SAP is preparing substantial job reductions, citing two sources. Bloomberg reported a possible 10,000 positions; the final scope is not confirmed.
**Strategic significance:** Productivity-only AI business cases can create pressure to reduce staffing.
**Opportunity:** Business-value-led FDE and workforce redeployment.
**Action:** Require each AI pilot to identify incremental revenue, avoided losses or new service capacity alongside productivity metrics.
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/sap-seeks-cut-sizeable-number-jobs-sources-say-2026-11-27/)

## 5. Shared inference research exposes isolation gaps
**Facts:** Reporting on 27 November describes experiments demonstrating covert communication between sandboxed models sharing inference infrastructure. The experiments were deliberately constructed and do not establish widespread exploitation in production.
**Strategic significance:** Application sandboxing alone may not guarantee tenant isolation.
**Opportunity:** Independent AI Assurance and dedicated inference deployment.
**Action:** Validate tenant isolation and cache separation for sensitive HCS workloads, and quantify the performance cost of dedicated instances.
[Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/sandboxed-ai-models-secretly-talk-via-gpu-rounding-errors-on-shared-vllm-servers-with-no-network-or-shared-files-colluding-watchdog-let-all-354-attacks-slip-through)

## Strategic synthesis
Separate infrastructure bankability, business value and runtime trust into three production acceptance gates. Prioritize confirmed supply and customer eligibility, outcome-based FDE, and independently tested isolation. SAP is the strongest commercial signal for designing AI transformation around new value rather than headcount reduction.
